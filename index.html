Hey! I'm Thierry — a Visual Storyteller and competitive paraglider pilot from the Bernese Oberland. After two years at the communications agency Kornflex in Bern, where I worked on client projects from day one, I'm completing my apprenticeship in July 2026. Photo & video, motion design, branding and social media — that's my craft. When I'm not editing or designing, I'm in the air. I compete across Europe at cups and championships, logging 150–200 flight hours a year.
